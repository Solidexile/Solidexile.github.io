DropKeeper: delete your data

Last updated: 21 October 2026

DropKeeper keeps your business data on your phone, not on our servers, so you are always in control of deleting it.

Delete everything

  • On your phone: Android Settings → Apps → DropKeeper → Storage → Clear data, or
  • Uninstall DropKeeper. Android deletes all of its data with it.

Instagram data

We don't keep Instagram data on any server. The temporary sign-in token on our server is deleted within 10 minutes of connecting. To remove the connection:

  • In DropKeeper: Settings → Live feed → Disconnect deletes the token from your phone.
  • In Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites, then remove DropKeeper.
