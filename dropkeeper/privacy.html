DropKeeper privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026 · Published by Solidify

Short version: DropKeeper has no accounts and no ads, and doesn't track you. Your sales, products, customers and numbers stay on your phone. We never see them.

Data you enter

Products, stock, sales, customers (names, phone numbers, addresses you choose to add), expenses and listing views are stored only in a database on your device. They are not uploaded to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Backups and exports are files that you create and choose where to save or share.

Instagram connection (optional, Pro)

If you connect an Instagram Business or Creator account, you sign in on Instagram's own page. To finish the sign-in, Instagram sends a login code to our small server (hosted on Supabase), which exchanges it for an access token and passes the token back to your phone. The token is held on our server for at most 10 minutes, then deleted. It is deleted as soon as your phone collects it. After that, the token is stored encrypted on your phone only. The app uses it to read your own posts and their statistics (views, likes, comments, saves) directly from Instagram. We don't store your posts, statistics or profile on any server. You can disconnect at any time in the app; you can also remove DropKeeper's access in Instagram's settings under Apps and websites.

Receipts

When you send a receipt, DropKeeper creates the PDF on your phone and hands it to WhatsApp or another app you choose. Nothing is sent until you press Send in that app.

Exchange rates

The app downloads public currency rates from open.er-api.com. This request contains no personal data.

Purchases

DropKeeper Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app only checks with Google Play whether Pro is owned on your Google account.

Permissions

  • Photos: only when you pick a picture for a custom background. A copy of that picture is kept inside the app.
  • Notifications: for reminders inside the app.
  • Internet: for exchange rates, the Instagram connection and Google Play.

Children

DropKeeper is a business tool for sellers and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

All your data is on your phone: clear it in Android Settings → Apps → DropKeeper → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. See how to delete your data.
