Your data

Your shop's data is stored on your phone. Keep backups (Settings → Backup): if your phone is lost or the app is uninstalled without a backup, we can't recover it. See the privacy policy for what little reaches our server.

DropKeeper Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase through Google Play that unlocks the live feed, tracked links and custom backgrounds. Payment, refunds and restoring the purchase are handled by Google Play under its own terms. Connections to other platforms depend on those platforms keeping their services available to us; if one withdraws access, that connection may stop working.
