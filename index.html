Selected work

What it looks like when we finish something.

PathKeeper

Speak a thought. It becomes a task, a note, an expense, or a project log.

Say “call the garage tomorrow at 9am and pick up the parts on Friday” and you get two tasks, each with its own reminder, at the times you actually said. Mention a project by name and the entry files itself under it. It splits lists the way a person would — not every “and” starts a new item.

There is no account and no cloud. Everything lives in a database on the device, and dictation is handled by the phone itself, so nothing is uploaded anywhere. Written in English, French and Arabic.

Download the beta Android 7+ · 50 MB

Direct download while PathKeeper finishes Google Play review. · Privacy policy

Android shows two warnings for any app not installed from the Play Store. They are expected here — PathKeeper is not on Play yet. Here is what you will see:

  1. “This type of file can harm your device” — tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the downloaded file, from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. “For your security, you can't install unknown apps from this source” — tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press back.
  4. Tap Install.
  5. If Play Protect says “unsafe app blocked” or offers to scan, choose More details → Install anyway.
  6. Open PathKeeper. Nothing else is needed — there is no account to make.

Wording varies a little by phone; Samsung says “Install unknown apps”. Stuck on any step? Send a screenshot to hwmoubarak@gmail.com.
